4. BONUS INVENTORY

This is critical.

A bonus product is free in price, but it is still a physical unit leaving branch inventory.

Example:
Buy 10 + 1 free

The operational inventory must account for all 11 units that are actually supplied.

Review:
- branch_allocate_order RPC
- Branch Manager allocation logic
- inventory table
- order_items.is_bonus
- financial inventory movement

Implement the safest solution that preserves existing data and business rules.

Do NOT double-deduct inventory.

The final behavior must be auditable:
- paid quantity
- bonus quantity
- total physical quantity supplied

must remain distinguishable.
